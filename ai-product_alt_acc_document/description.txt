Product AI Alternate & Accessory | AI-Powered Product Suggestion & Catalog Enrichment


The Product AI Alternate & Accessory module provides a complete solution for automatically identifying and applying alternative and accessory products across your Odoo catalog using the power of OpenAI. Instead of manually linking related products one by one, your team selects the products they want to analyse, configures a few settings, and lets the AI do the work — scanning your full catalog, pricing data, categories, tags, attributes, and real sales history to generate intelligent suggestions that actually reflect how your customers shop.

The entire process is managed through a guided six-step dashboard that walks your team from product selection all the way through to reviewing and applying AI suggestions directly to your Odoo product records. Products can be selected individually through a live search, added all at once with a single click, or imported in bulk by uploading an Excel file containing SKU codes. A downloadable reference template ensures your team always uploads files in the correct format. Once products are selected and settings are configured, the system generates a structured Excel data file and sends it to OpenAI along with your business context, after which the results appear in a searchable review table ready to be applied in one click.

Every analysis run is fully logged with the status, error details if anything went wrong, suggestion type used, confidence level, total tokens consumed, and a per-product breakdown of exactly what alternatives and accessories were applied. The entire flow can also be automated through Odoo cron jobs so your product relationships stay current without any manual effort.

1. Features

AI-Powered Product Suggestions:
The AI analyses your product catalog, pricing, categories, tags, attributes, and sales history together and returns structured alternatives and accessories for every selected product — no manual matching, no guesswork.

Smart Product Selection:
Add products to your analysis individually through a live search, select your entire active catalog in one click, or upload a bulk SKU file to import hundreds of products at once without having to search one by one.

SKU File Import:
Upload an Excel file containing Internal Reference codes and the system automatically matches each SKU against your Odoo product catalog and adds the matched products to your selection instantly.
Reference Template Download:
Download a pre-formatted Excel template with the correct column structure so your team always uploads files in the right format without any confusion about which columns are required.

Flexible AI Configuration:
Control exactly how the AI behaves — choose between alternatives only, accessories only, or both, set a price tolerance percentage for alternative matching, define the maximum number of suggestions per product, and select the minimum confidence level to filter out weak matches.

Excel Data Preview:
Before running the AI, the system generates a structured Excel file containing your selected products, the full product catalog, and sales history — available to download so your team can verify the data going into the analysis.

AI Result Review:
All suggestions are displayed in a clean paginated table with search so your team can review every alternative and accessory for every product before committing to apply anything.

One-Click Apply:
Once your team is satisfied with the suggestions, a single click writes all alternatives and accessories directly to the corresponding Odoo product records — no manual editing required.

Detailed AI Logs:
Every analysis run creates a log entry recording the status, error message if any, suggestion type, confidence level, total tokens used, and a full per-product breakdown of what was suggested and what was applied, giving you a complete audit trail.

Auto-Apply via Cron:
Configure the system to run analysis automatically on a schedule and apply results without any manual intervention, so your product relationships stay up to date even as your catalog grows and changes.

Guided Six-Step Dashboard:
A clear step-by-step interface walks your team through the entire workflow — from entering business context and selecting products, through configuration and data preview, to running the AI and reviewing results — so nothing gets missed.

Live Status Tracking:
Each analysis card shows a real-time status badge showing whether the card is Idle, Running, Done, or in Error, so your team always knows the current state at a glance.

Business Context Input:
Provide a plain-language description of your business so the AI understands your industry and product relationships, making suggestions more relevant to how your specific customers actually think and shop.

Automatic Error Logging:
When anything goes wrong — whether an API timeout, a connection error, a missing configuration, or an unexpected response — the exact error is captured in the AI log with a clear message so your team knows precisely what happened and what to fix.

FAQs

Q1. What does the AI Product Alternate & Accessory module do?
This module uses Artificial Intelligence to automatically analyse your product catalog and recommend the best alternative products and accessories for each product. It saves you hours of manual work by building product relationships intelligently based on your catalog data, product attributes, and real sales history.


Q2. Which AI models are supported in this module?
The module supports multiple OpenAI models including GPT-4.1, GPT-4.1 Mini, GPT-5, GPT-5 Mini, GPT-5.4, and GPT-5.4 Mini. You can select the model that best fits your business needs from the Configuration page.


Q3. Where do I enter my OpenAI API key?
You can enter your OpenAI API key from the Configuration menu inside the AI Dashboard. Simply open the Configuration page, enter your ChatGPT Key, select your preferred LLM model, and save.


Q4. What is the difference between Alternative Products and Accessory Products?
Alternative products are products that a customer would buy instead of the selected product, meaning they serve a similar purpose. Accessory products are products that a customer would buy together with the selected product, meaning they complement each other. The AI recommends both types based on your catalog and sales data.


Q5. Do I need to select products manually every time I run the analysis?
No. You can either search and select products individually, use the Select All option to include your entire catalog, or upload an Excel file with product internal references to bulk-select products. If you enable the Scheduled Auto Run option, the system handles everything automatically on a daily basis without any manual selection.


Q6. Is the Business Info description mandatory?
No, it is optional but strongly recommended. Adding a business description improves the accuracy of the AI suggestions significantly, especially for businesses with specialized or niche product catalogs.


Q7. How many days of Sales History does the system use?
You can configure the number of days of past sales data to include using the Sales History Days field in the Configuration step. By default it is set to 30 days, but you can increase or decrease this based on how much historical data you want the AI to consider.


Q8. Can I review the AI suggestions before applying them to the products?
Yes. Step 6 — Review & Apply shows you all the AI recommendations in a clear table before anything is saved. You can review each product's suggested alternatives and accessories, search for specific products, and only apply them when you are satisfied with the results.


Q10. How do I track how many API tokens are being used?
Every AI log record shows the Total Tokens Used for that particular run. You can refer to this figure to monitor your API consumption and plan your OpenAI usage accordingly.
